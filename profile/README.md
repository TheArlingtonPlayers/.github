Welcome to the engineering and technology organization for **[The Arlington Players](https://thearlingtonplayers.org)**.

Founded in 1951, TAP is an award-winning, all-volunteer 501(c)(3) nonprofit community theatre based in Arlington County, Virginia, producing major musicals and plays at the Thomas Jefferson Community Theatre.

---

## 🌟 Mission & Vision

- **Our Mission:** To produce epic and artistically challenging theatre by cultivating an environment of excellence and education for all of our members, volunteers, and audiences in the communities of Arlington County, VA and the greater DC area.
- **Our Vision:** To foster a community where individuals of all backgrounds can explore, learn, and experience all elements of theatrical arts.

---

## 🎭 Technology Behind the Curtain

Just as our actors, designers, and crew bring stories to life on stage, our volunteer technology team builds and maintains the digital infrastructure supporting TAP's ticketing, web presence, reimbursements, archives, and production operations.

Our technology practices directly reflect TAP's core organizational values:

* **Professional Quality & Excellence:** We commit to a high standard of engineering—using Infrastructure as Code, continuous integration, containerization, and modern web frameworks.
* **Fiscal Responsibility & Sustainability:** In alignment with our value to maintain fiscal stability, our cloud architecture is intentionally designed for zero-waste, leveraging Google Cloud free tiers and serverless compute to keep recurring hosting costs under \$15/month.
* **Removing Barriers & Promoting Inclusion:** We build accessible, responsive, and intuitive tools that ensure everyone in our community can engage with our productions, onstage and off.
* **Education & Volunteer Growth:** We provide a welcoming environment where tech contributors can learn new tools, experiment with modern stacks, and develop practical software and DevOps skills.

---

## 🛠️ Our Tech Stack

* **Cloud & Foundation:** Google Cloud Platform (GCP) managed via **Terraform** & **OpenTofu**.
* **Public Web & CMS:** **Python 3**, **Django**, and **Wagtail CMS** running containerized on **Cloud Run** with **Cloud SQL (PostgreSQL)** and Google Cloud Storage.
* **Volunteer Operations:** Custom internal web tooling (PHP 8.x) for volunteer production reimbursements, secured with Google Identity-Aware Proxy (IAP).
* **CI/CD & Security:** Keyless automated deployments with **GitHub Actions** via **Workload Identity Federation (WIF)** and centralized authentication with Google Workspace.
